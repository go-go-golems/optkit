# Layer Sections and Variable Registry for Candidate Proposals

## Overview

OPTKIT-011 is the umbrella design ticket for a modular optimization workbench. It records the original intern design, screen-level product vision, architect review, and authoritative backend-first program roadmap. Implementation is split into OPTKIT-012 through OPTKIT-020 so each major contract receives focused investigation, review, implementation, and validation without losing the end-to-end product flow.
